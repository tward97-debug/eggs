# Planned changes

Notes from Kaleb, 2026-11-17. Nothing here is built yet.

## Habitat and shipping menus

- A slot should offer every upgrade option, and any option you can afford
  should be buyable, including skipping tiers.
- Bug: the upgrade buttons do not become clickable when you gain enough cash
  while the menu is open. They only update after the menu is closed and
  reopened.

## Research

- Press and hold a research button to keep buying levels.

## Garden

- Add a trophy button on the Garden page that shows progress toward each corn
  goal (the harvest counts that unlock the next crop and pay the one-time
  reward).

## Drone rework

- One second before a drone appears, show a small red dot where the drone
  button will be.
- The drone button then appears at a random spot in the blue (sky) area,
  instead of always in the same place.
- Drones appear 5 times a minute on average (currently about every 15
  seconds).
- A drone reward is 20 times the current earnings per second (currently a
  small fraction of farm value).

## Name

- Rename the game from "Egg Empire" to "Chickenville".
- Open point: the Android app id is `com.triston.eggempire`. Changing it would
  make phones treat the game as a new app and lose the old save, so the plan
  is to change only the visible name unless decided otherwise.
